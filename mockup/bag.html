What we don't tell you

We measure. We don't review — and those are genuinely different jobs.

Whether a bag survives five years, whether the wheels cope with cobblestones, whether the warranty gets honoured, whether the handle rattles — none of that shows up on a tape measure. We have nothing useful to say about any of it.
