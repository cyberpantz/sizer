## Selection and limits

The Travelpro entries are approximately 55 × 40 × 20 cm; the Briggs & Riley entries are 53.3 × 38.1 × 22.9 cm. Neither shape fits every airline envelope: width and depth constraints differ. These observations are arithmetic comparisons with example envelopes, not fresh airline policy audits. Fare entitlement and packed weight remain separate checks. No claim of free carriage on basic fares, underseat fit, regional-aircraft accommodation, or guaranteed boarding is made.

The Maxlite 5 International spinner already existed and was not counted again. The added Rollaboard is a distinct two-wheel configuration.
