Latest `npm test`: 327 tests, 323 passed, 4 failed. The failures expose test assumptions incompatible with the brief; no source numbers were changed to satisfy them:

1. `bag-merge.test.mjs`: expects every manufacturer record to have `external_cm`; claimed-only intake is explicitly allowed by the brief. BÉIS instead has sourced `claimed_cm`.

4. `unit-intake.test.mjs`: expects build-conversion metadata on that same manufacturer-published metric figure. It was not converted and must not be labelled as such.
