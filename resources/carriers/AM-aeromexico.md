# AM — Aeroméxico allowance research

Verified 2026-09-01 from Aeroméxico's official [baggage page](https://beta.aeromexico.com/en-us/travel-information/baggage), [fare families](https://beta.aeromexico.com/en-us/travel-information/fare-types-and-tickets), and [onboard baggage guide](https://www.aeromexico.com/es-mx/blog-de-viajes-inspirate/especificaciones-equipaje-a-bordo).

Light excludes carry-on and offers it for a fee. Basic includes one carry-on,
with a 10 kg combined allowance across carry-on and personal item. Carry-on is
55 × 40 × 25 cm including wheels and handle. Aeroméxico calls 45 × 35 × 20 cm
and 3 kg a recommendation for the personal item, so those figures are not
stored as mandatory maximums.
