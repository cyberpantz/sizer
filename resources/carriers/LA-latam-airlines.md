# LA — LATAM Airlines allowance research

Verified 2026-09-01 from LATAM's official pages:

- Dimensions: https://www.latamairlines.com/gb/en/help-center/faq/baggage/included/dimensions-weight
- Fare inclusion: https://www.latamairlines.com/us/en/help-center/faq/purchases/asistance/ticket-fares

LATAM publishes a 45 × 35 × 20 cm small bag or backpack, including pockets,
wheels and handle, with a 10 kg limit. Economy carry-on is 55 × 35 × 25 cm and
12 kg. Basic on flights within Chile, Peru, Colombia and Ecuador includes the
small bag but excludes the suitcase; Light includes carry-on.

The schema cannot scope a fare to individual countries. Checked inclusion also
varies by fare and route, so it remains unresearched rather than flattened.
