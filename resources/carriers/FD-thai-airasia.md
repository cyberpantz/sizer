# FD — Thai AirAsia allowance research

Verified 2026-09-01 from AirAsia's official Thai-language support page:
https://support.airasia.com/s/article/What-are-the-rules-for-cabin-baggage-on-board?language=th

## Cabin and personal item

- One cabin bag may measure up to 56 × 36 × 23 cm, including its handles,
  wheels and side pockets.
- One laptop bag, handbag, backpack or other small personal item may measure up
  to 40 × 30 × 10 cm and must fit beneath the seat.
- The two pieces share a combined 7 kg allowance.

The page identifies rules and ancillary-baggage information for Thai AirAsia
(`FD`) and Thai AirAsia X (`XJ`). Only `FD` is represented by this record.

## Unmodeled or unresolved

- Xtra Carry-On and Fast Pass are paid entitlements and are not part of Low
  Fare. They require add-on-aware booking data before activation.
- Checked baggage is sold in variable weight bands. It was not flattened into a
  free checked allowance.
- Infants receive no cabin allowance, but infant travel is outside the current
  standard-adult scope.
- No physical sizer dimensions or independent enforcement evidence were found.
