Verified 2026-09-01 from Avianca's official pages:

- Carry-on: https://ayuda.avianca.com/hc/en-us/articles/13080259544219-What-is-carry-on-baggage-and-how-can-I-purchase-it
- Personal item: https://ayuda.avianca.com/hc/en-us/articles/13080081545243-What-is-a-personal-item
- 2026 fare change: https://www.avianca.com/en/about-us/corporate-news/2026/february-2

Carry-on is 55 × 35 × 25 cm including wheels and handles, with a 10 kg limit.
The personal item is 45 × 35 × 20 cm and is included in every fare. From 27
January 2026, Light on international flights within the Americas includes both;
domestic Basic continues to include only the personal item, with carry-on sold
separately.

Checked baggage and fare families beyond this explicit scope remain
unresearched. The purchase-date transition is preserved in the record note.
