# TG — Thai Airways allowance research

Verified 2026-09-01 from Thai Airways' official website.

## Cabin and personal item

Source: https://www.thaiairways.com/en-lk/content/baggage/carry-on-baggage/

- Economy passengers receive one cabin bag up to 56 × 45 × 25 cm and 7 kg.
- Thai Airways says the dimensions include wheels, handles and side pockets.
- One free handbag, wallet, purse, notebook or portable computer may measure up
  to 37.5 × 25 × 12.5 cm (or 75 cm total) and weigh up to 1.5 kg.
- The page warns that spot checks may be performed with a test unit or scale at
  check-in or the boarding gate. This documents possible checks, but is not
  evidence for a researched enforcement score.

## Checked baggage

Source: https://www.thaiairways.com/en-gb/content/baggage/checked-baggage/

For international travel commencing on or after 2 March 2026, each Economy bag
may weigh up to 23 kg and measure up to 158 cm in total, including wheels and
handles. Economy Standard and Saver generally receive one piece; Flexi and Full
Flex receive two. Saver has destination exceptions, including some tickets with
no checked allowance, so this record activates only the unambiguous Economy
Standard row.

## Unmodeled or unresolved

- Checked allowance depends on fare, route, ticket issue date and travel date.
- Star Alliance and Royal Orchid Plus status can add pieces.
- Premium Economy, Royal Silk and Royal First entitlements were visible but are
  outside this record's standard-Economy scope.
- No physical sizer dimensions were published.
