# G3 — GOL Linhas Aéreas allowance research

Verified 2026-09-01 from GOL's official [baggage page](https://www.voegol.com.br/en-US/web/guest/nh/bagagens) and [fare table](https://www.voegol.com.br/en/nh/smiles2-dvn).

For tickets purchased from 14 October 2025, Basic includes only a 43 × 32 × 22
cm personal item up to 10 kg. Its 55 × 35 × 25 cm, 12 kg overhead bag is paid.
Light, Classic, Flex and Premium Economy include both. The overhead measurements
include wheels and handles. Older tickets retain the earlier 10 kg policy.
