# CM — Copa Airlines allowance research

Verified 2026-09-01 from Copa's official June 2025 carry-on policy update:
https://www.copaair.com/assets/carry-on-baggage-policy-update.pdf

Main Cabin and Business passengers receive one 56 × 36 × 26 cm cabin bag, up
to 118 cm linear and 10 kg. All passengers receive one 43 × 25 × 22 cm personal
item, up to 90 cm linear, beneath the seat. Copa publishes inches and metric;
both are retained without reconciling their rounding.

Checked inclusion varies by fare and remains outside this pass. The policy says
Copa may impose additional limits when circumstances require, but that is not a
measurable enforcement rule or a physical sizer dimension.
